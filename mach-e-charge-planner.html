Practical Tips for This Route

  • Home charging both ends is the winning strategy — leave with enough to arrive at 15–25% and top up overnight.
  • In cold weather, precondition the cabin and battery while still plugged in. This helps range significantly.
  • Highway speeds above 70–72 mph hurt efficiency noticeably. 65–70 is a good balance.
  • Sioux City is the most useful midpoint if you ever need a top-up. Council Bluffs is excellent as a backup near Omaha.
  • Keep a CCS adapter (and NACS if you use Tesla stations) in the car.
